Maven Academy, pass 3: heavier where it's earned, quiet everywhere else

This audit covers index.html (the landing page) and welcome.html (the page people see after paying). In pass 2 the direction changed to heavier, more physical interactions, so Jhey's playful lens now leads, Jakub keeps it polished, and Emil applies only where motion slows down the path to Reserve. The fixes from pass 1 were built with more weight, and pass 3 closed every remaining finding. Each one below is marked with the pass that resolved it.

Overall

Pass 2 puts the weight where people actually interact. The FAQ, the chat, the Vault cards and the seat bar now move with real mass, and the welcome page finally celebrates the payment. Reduced-motion support is now complete. Pass 3 removed the uninvited motion: the intro plays once per visitor, the chat never opens on its own, the pulses are gone, and the loops sleep when nothing is moving. Weight now only shows up where someone asked for it.

What changed in pass 3

The eight findings left open after pass 2 are now built and checked in Chrome (first visit, return visit, chat, FAQ). Every change has a reduced-motion fallback.

  • ✓Intro once per visitor (finding 5). The loader and guide play on the first visit only. A tiny inline script in <head> reads maven-intro-seen before first paint, so returning visitors never see a flash of the loader.
  • ✓No uninvited chat (finding 7). After 15s idle the launcher nudges once (900ms --heavy double bounce) instead of opening the panel. Clicking the background no longer opens the chat or the facilitator; it only closes whatever is open.
  • ✓No endless pulses (finding 9). The hero dot is still. The seat counter's live dot plays one 1.1s ping when the data arrives, then stops.
  • ✓Reveal with meaning (finding 4). Down from 37 blocks to 13 section heads plus 5 signature blocks. Heads rise 28px over 900ms with --spring; signature blocks land from 64px with a slight scale and --heavy, so the two read differently.
  • ✓Performance (finding 10). The follower and guide bubble move with transform and the translate property, not left/top. The brick field sleeps after about 30 calm frames and wakes on mouse movement. The Vault gradients drift an oversized layer with transform instead of repainting background-position.
  • ✓Quieter close. The chat and facilitator modal open with weight (450–500ms --heavy) and close in 180–200ms with a shorter travel.
  • ✓Marquee pauses on hover and keyboard focus (WCAG 2.2.2).
  • ✓One easing feel. No bare ease is left in index.html or welcome.html.

What changed in pass 2

Built to the new direction: heavier and more physical, but only on things the visitor triggers or the rare moments that have earned it. Every new animation has a reduced-motion fallback in the same code.

  • ✓New easing token. --heavy: cubic-bezier(.34,1.42,.52,1) adds mass and a small overshoot for weighted moments, alongside the existing --ease and --spring. index.html:47
  • ✓FAQ (finding 2). The answer height animates over 550ms using ::details-content and interpolate-size. The answer text drops in and comes into focus, the plus icon turns 135° with an overshoot, and the open card lifts −3px onto its hard shadow.
  • ✓Chat (finding 3). A three-dot typing indicator shows for 800ms, then each bubble lands from its own corner (scaling up from .86 and coming into focus) over 550ms with --heavy. The panel smooth-scrolls to the newest message.
  • ✓Vault (finding 6). Hover spins are gone. The card now tilts toward the cursor (up to ±12° and ±16°) like a physical object, and click, tap or Enter does a weighted 180° flip over 900ms. Focus alone no longer animates.
  • ✓Reduced motion (finding 1). Smooth scrolling, scrollIntoView and the bottom card now respect the preference, along with every new animation.
  • ✓Seat bar. When the meter scrolls into view, taken seats drop in one at a time (45ms apart, 600ms each, --heavy).
  • ✓Maven reacts to Reserve. Hovering any Reserve button makes the cursor mascot hop with a 700ms tilt.
  • ✓welcome.html celebration. Maven drops in from above, 12 sparks burst outward, the card lands with a slight rotation, then the chip, headline, steps and WhatsApp button settle in order over about 2.3s. It plays once on arrival. welcome.html:40
  • ◐Accepted under the new direction. The bottom card keeps its overshoot (finding 8), since bounce now fits the brief. The scroll reveal was made heavier (40px over 900ms), but it's still the same on all 37 blocks, so finding 4 stays open.

Where the timings land

Pass 2 deliberately moved the interactions into the slower zones: the FAQ, chat and Vault now take 550–900ms because the brief asks for weight. They're fine there because the visitor triggers each one. The loader (9) is the exception: it's still slow and still unprompted.

Instant / Responsive · 0–300ms: hovers, presses, toggles, small panels.
Deliberate · 300–500ms: earned by modals and large surfaces.
Sluggish · 500ms+: only for rare, cinematic moments.
What's off

The red zone is now intentional: the FAQ (10), chat (11) and Vault flip (8) are slow because the visitor chose to trigger them, and the brief asks for weight. Two don't fit that rule: the loader (9), which runs uninvited on every load, and the fade-up (7), which runs on all 37 blocks.

Resolved · pass 2CriticalJakubAccessibility

Two motions ignore reduced motion: smooth scrolling and the bouncing bottom card

Whathtml{scroll-behavior: smooth} and the facilitator modal's scrollIntoView({behavior:"smooth"}) run for everyone. On a 2,000-line page, that's a long glide across the whole page. The bottom card's overshooting slide-up (#bottom-bounce-card) also has no reduced-motion rule.

Why it mattersLong programmatic scrolls and overshooting motion are the kinds of motion that cause dizziness for people with vestibular sensitivity, and those are exactly the users who set the preference. Everything else on the page respects it, so these two gaps stand out.

Built in pass 2Done exactly as recommended, plus reduced-motion fallbacks for every new pass-2 animation. Original recommendation: add html{scroll-behavior:auto} and #bottom-bounce-card{transition:opacity .15s linear} inside the existing reduced-motion block. In JS, use behavior: reduce ? "auto" : "smooth". When motion is reduced, things should just fade in, with no movement.

WhatThe seven <details> items use the browser's default open and close, which happen instantly. The one-open-at-a-time script closes the previous answer at the same moment the new one opens, so the content below jumps by two answers' worth of height at once.

Why it mattersThe FAQ is where hesitant buyers settle their doubts about price, tools and recordings. A layout jump right there makes the visitor lose their place on the page, just when they're deciding.

Built in pass 2Heavier version: 550ms --spring height, answer drops 10px and comes into focus, the plus turns 135° with --heavy, and the open card lifts −3px. Resolved · pass 3CriticalEmilJakub

A full-screen loader and darkened guide overlay block the page on every refresh

WhatThe loader stays up for 2,100ms on desktop (1,400ms on mobile), fades over 550ms, then 650ms later a dimmed, blurred overlay appears with the guide bubble. The overlay stays until the visitor clicks. The code says it runs on "every refresh", for returning visitors too.

Why it mattersThe page is a static file and is ready almost instantly, so the loader isn't waiting for anything. It's about 3 seconds plus a forced click before anyone can read or reach Reserve. Visitors coming back to pay, the people most likely to convert, sit through it every time.

Recommended motionShow the full sequence once per visitor (store a flag with localStorage, wrapped in try/catch). After that, let the page appear right away. Even on the first visit, shorten it: Maven settles in 400ms and the loader fades over 300ms, about 0.8s in total. Replace the darkened overlay with a non-blocking bubble.

Built in pass 3The loader and guide now run once per visitor. A <head> script checks localStorage before first paint and hides the loader for returning visitors; the flag is set when the first full sequence finishes.

Resolved · pass 2ImportantEmil

Vault cards spin 180° on hover and spin again on leave, and keyboard focus does the same

Whatmouseenter and mouseleave each add 180° over 720ms, so moving the cursor across the five cards sets off up to ten 3D rotations. focus and blur do the same, so tabbing through the Vault spins every card twice.

Why it mattersA hover should be passive feedback, not a 0.7-second event. Spinning is also a known motion-sickness trigger. Even though reduced motion turns it off, everyone else still gets spins they never asked for, including keyboard users, whose actions shouldn't trigger animation.

Built in pass 2Hover tilts the card toward the cursor; click, tap or Enter flips it over 900ms with --heavy; focus alone does nothing. Original recommendation: make the flip something you choose: hover only lifts the card (the existing shadow change), and a click, tap or Enter flips it 180° over 450ms. The same action flips it back. On keyboard focus, show the back with no animation.

The chat opens by itself: after 15s idle, or on any click on the page background

WhatAfter 15 seconds without input, the chat panel slides open uninvited. Separately, any single click on empty background opens the chat 260ms later, and a double-click opens the facilitator modal.

Why it mattersFifteen seconds without input usually means someone is reading, and a panel sliding over the copy interrupts them. People also click the background all the time to focus the window or clear a text selection, so the chat pops up when nobody meant to open it. Motion should answer what the visitor did, not ambush them.

Recommended motionRemove the background-click and idle auto-open. Instead, after idling, give the "Ask Maven" button a single small two-hop nudge (4px) once per visit. The visitor decides whether to open the chat.

Built in pass 3Idle no longer opens the chat; the launcher nudges once per visit instead. Background click and double-click no longer open anything; click-away still closes the chat or modal.

Resolved · pass 3ImportantEmilPerformance

Animation loops that never pause, and a follower moved by left/top

WhatThe brick-field loop redraws every brick on every frame forever, even after you've scrolled past the hero and the bricks have stopped moving. The cursor follower and guide bubble set style.left/top each frame. Five Vault cards animate background-position, which forces the browser to repaint them on every frame. That's on top of a full-screen WebGL shader and an 80px backdrop-filter strip fixed to the bottom of the screen.

Pass 2More urgent now. Heavier motion makes dropped frames more noticeable, and the Vault tilt adds 3D transforms while the gradient loops repaint underneath.

Why it mattersOn a mid-range laptop all of this adds up to dropped frames and a hot fan while someone is just reading. Moving elements with left/top forces the browser to recalculate layout every frame, while transform is handled cheaply by the graphics card.

Recommended motionMove the follower with transform: translate3d(x,y,0). Stop the brick loop once every brick has settled, and stop it while the hero is off-screen (an IntersectionObserver can detect this). Drift the Vault gradients by moving an oversized ::before layer with transform instead of animating background-position.

Built in pass 3Follower and guide bubble moved to transform/translate; brick loop sleeps once calm and wakes on mouse movement; Vault gradients drift with transform on an oversized layer.
