## Week 1 · Me, live (16–22 Nov)

**Day 1 · Mon 16 Nov**
🔥 *Day 1/30 · Week 1: Me, live*
This week you build your own one-page site: who you are, what you do, how to reach you.
*Today (30 min):* write a 3-line brief.
1. Who should visit your page?
2. What should they understand in 5 seconds?
3. What should they do next (WhatsApp you, see your work, book you)?
*Post:* Day 1 · my brief

**Day 2 · Tue 17 Nov**

**Day 3 · Wed 18 Nov**

**Day 4 · Thu 19 Nov**

**Day 5 · Fri 20 Nov**

## Week 2 · A real business (23–29 Nov)
