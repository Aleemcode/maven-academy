Reframe

It's a guessing machine.

When you tell an AI to "make a notes screen", it evaluates the probability of every token in its corpus. Because 90% of notes apps online look like bootstrap gray lists, it delivers the exact center of that bell curve.

"It works" is the machine's finish line. "It's right" is yours. You do not win by typing code faster — you win by narrowing the guess with design tokens.

Taste Foundation

Essential Design Lingo (Lesson 1.1)

Negative Space
The deliberate void surrounding elements that gives an interface breathing room and gravitas.
Tactile Shadow
Hard 100% black offsets (5px 5px 0) that ground cards into physical, tactile planes rather than muddy blurs.
Optical Balance
Adjusting elements so they appear centered to the human eye, even when mathematical coordinates differ.
