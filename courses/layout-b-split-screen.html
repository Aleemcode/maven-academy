Probabilistic Foundations

The Three Laws of AI Guessing

The machine does not understand software architecture or aesthetic tension. It predicts the most mathematically probable token sequence based on billions of pages of internet code. Three inescapable consequences fall out of that:

01
It drifts to the mathematical middle: Without high-fidelity design constraints, you get the average of every mediocre SaaS dashboard ever created.
02
It can be fluent and dead wrong simultaneously: A perfectly indented Tailwind component can completely break accessibility or visual hierarchy without warning.
03
Its confidence tells you nothing: The model answers with identical authoritative syntax whether it is generating production code or utter slop. Only your trained eye governs quality.

Build-Along Recipe · 1-Click Senior XML
Google Stitch

Step 1: Open Stitch in your browser. Copy and run this directed XML prompt: 

<screen_request>
  <role>Lead Warm Fusion UI Architect</role>
  <objective>Design a calm, minimalist notes app screen that feels like a blank sheet of archival paper.</objective>
  
  <spatial_rules>
    - Generous negative space (min 64px padding on desktop).
    - One centered, borderless input with subtle caret.
    - Single muted timestamp in --wf-f-mono (Space Mono).
  </spatial_rules>

  <design_tokens>
    - Background: --wf-ground (#F4F0E5)
    - Surface: --wf-surface (#FFFFFF)
    - Text: --wf-ink (#14261D)
    - Accent: --wf-terra (#E8722C)
    - No gratuitous gradients or arbitrary hex values.
  </design_tokens>
</screen_request>
What Stitch Outputs: A restrained, tactile notes card with breathing room. Notice how specifying negative constraints killed the floating cards and blue gradients before Stitch could generate them.
