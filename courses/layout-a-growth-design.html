3 seconds later
You type "build me a notes app" and hit enter. Something appears. It looks… fine. Familiar. Exactly like every other notes app. That "fine" is the trap.

The machine didn't understand you. It doesn't know things. It predicts the next likely word, then the next — from patterns across everything it ever read. Three things fall out of that:

1
It aims for the most likely answer — so it drifts to the average of everything.
2
It can be fluent and wrong at once — and won't warn you.
3
Its confidence tells you nothing. Only your judgment does.
Quick one — predict:

You give the same tool a vague prompt, then a specific one. What changes the result more?

Yes — your words

Same machine, same second. The only thing that changed was how much you told it. A guesser guesses average when you give it little, and guesses toward you when you give it a lot. You're about to watch it happen.
