## Voice & No-Go Cheat-Sheet

1. **Sound like Aleem, not a brochure.** First person, warm, direct. Speak to one curious person, not "everyone."
2. **Be concrete over clever.** Name the real thing (the tool, the project, the day, the price). Specifics build trust; adjectives don't.
3. **Honesty is the brand.** Design-led with front-end fluency, not a backend engineer. Never overclaim. The taste-and-craft standard is the whole selling point.
4. **Banned words and tics:** supercharge, unlock, world-class, game-changer, revolutionary, empower, seamless, elevate, "in today's fast-paced world." Avoid em-dash saturation and stacked hype.
5. **No slop signals:** no stock-photo gloss, no decorative gradients, no emoji spam, no exclamation pile-ups. Whitespace and one confident accent beat visual noise.
6. **Every post earns its CTA.** Value or story first, then one clear ask. On terracotta, text is always forest, never white (contrast).
