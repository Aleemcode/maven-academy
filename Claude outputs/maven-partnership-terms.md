# Maven Builder Academy — Partner Agreement (Draft Terms)

*Prepared for Aleem Akinyoola · 1 October 2026 · Draft for discussion, not a signed contract*

> **Note:** This is a plain-English terms sheet to shape the deal and start the conversation — not legal advice. Before anyone signs, have a lawyer review the IP and licensing clauses in particular. The two tracks below are alternatives; a partner picks one.

---

## The idea in one line

You own the course — the teaching, the taste, the Warm Fusion system, the Vault. A partner brings students through their own audience and brand. You share the money per student. Nobody buys your decade of work for the price of a weekend.

There are two ways a partner can run with it. **Track A is where every partner starts.** Track B only opens once they've earned it.

---

## Track A — Co-branded partnership *(recommended starting point)*

The partner sells the cohort to their own audience. The learner sees the partner's brand front and centre, with a **"Powered by Maven Builder Academy"** credit. You teach it. You provide everything.

**Economics**

- Price per seat: **₦20,000** (founding-cohort price, set by Maven).
- Split: **60% Maven / 40% Partner**, per paid seat.
- Worked example — 100 students: ₦2,000,000 total → **₦1,200,000 to you**, ₦800,000 to the partner.

**Who does what**

- **Maven (you):** delivers all live sessions, the curriculum, the recordings, the Warm Fusion design system, the Design.md files, the Vault, the mentorship month, and a custom co-branded landing page for the partner to sell from.
- **Partner:** brings and markets to their own audience, owns the student relationship, collects payment, and handles their learners' questions and support.

**Money flow & trust**

- Payments land with the partner first (it's their storefront), so this clause matters: the partner remits your 60% **within 48 hours of each cohort's payment window closing**, or — cleaner — you split a shared Paystack sub-account so each side is paid automatically at checkout. Agree one of these before launch.
- A simple shared sheet logs every enrolment both sides can see. No guessing on headcount.

**IP & brand**

- All course IP — curriculum, Warm Fusion, Design.md files, Vault, recordings, mascot, page — **remains 100% Maven's property.** The partner gets a limited, revocable licence to *sell* this cohort under the co-brand, nothing more.
- The partner may not copy, teach, resell, or repackage the material outside this agreement.

**Term**

- Runs **per cohort**, renewed by mutual agreement each time. Either side can decline the next cohort with no penalty. Clean and low-risk for both.

## Track B — White-label licence *(only after a partner has earned it)*

This is the honest version of "sell it under your own brand and eventually run it yourself." The partner's learners never see the Maven name. But it's a **licence to teach — not a sale of ownership.** You keep the asset; the partner keeps their brand and most of the money.

**Gate — this track doesn't open on day one**

- A partner unlocks Track B only after **co-running 2–3 cohorts with you on Track A** and demonstrating they can deliver at your standard. This protects the method's reputation (and protects them from flopping their first solo run). Teaching this well is the whole product; it isn't handed over cold.

**Economics**

- **Upfront licence fee** — a real number, not a token. Starting point for discussion: **₦750,000–₦1,500,000** (you set it). This buys the right to run the course under their own brand for the licence term.
- **Ongoing royalty:** Maven keeps **25–30% per seat** for as long as the partner runs the course. The partner sets their own price to their own market and keeps the rest.
- Why both: the upfront fee values the system; the royalty keeps your interests aligned and your asset earning.

**IP & brand**

- All course IP **still remains 100% Maven's property.** Track B is a *licence to teach under a white label* — the partner never owns the curriculum, Warm Fusion, Design.md files, the Vault, or the recordings, and cannot sub-license, resell, or modify the method without written consent.
- The partner builds their own brand on the front; the engine underneath stays yours.

**Quality & protection**

- Maven may **audit a cohort** and may **revoke the licence** on: non-payment of royalties, dropping below an agreed delivery standard, or misuse of the brand/material.
- Licence is **non-exclusive** (you can have other partners and keep your own direct cohort) unless you deliberately grant exclusivity for a defined market/territory — and if you ever do, price it much higher.

**Term**

- **Annual, renewable.** Not perpetual. Renewal is a moment to re-check quality and terms.

**If a partner truly wants to OWN it outright**

- That's a separate conversation and a separate (much larger) number — a real valuation of a proven, revenue-generating course plus a decade of taste. Not ₦200k, and not while you're still the person who makes it work. Park it until the course demonstrably runs without you.

## Why not the original "₦200k + 10%, then you own it"

Kept here so the reasoning is on record:

- On a 100-seat run it nets ~₦400k total, then ownership transfers — **less than one co-branded cohort would pay you (₦1.2M)**, for permanent loss of the asset.
- "Own the course" would hand over the same system you use in your studio and your direct cohorts — arming a potential competitor with your own method.
- You *are* the product right now; a buyout prices the course as if it runs without you, which isn't true yet.
- Your "built with taste, not slop" reputation rides on delivery you'd no longer control.

Generosity to a brother is good. Generosity that gives away your moat isn't generosity — it's a loss you'll feel in a year. Track B lets you be generous *and* keep the asset.

---

## Suggested first step with this partner

Start on **Track A, co-branded, for one cohort.** Low risk, fast to launch, and it's the real test: can he actually bring students, and does he love delivering it alongside you? If yes, Track B is there to grow into. If not, you've lost nothing and kept everything.
