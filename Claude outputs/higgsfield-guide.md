# Maven × Higgsfield — how to spend your free credits well

The branded motion-graphics video (our MP4) is the backbone — clean, on-brand, fully in your control. Higgsfield is best used to add the **two things code can't do**: real 3D motion on Maven, and AI-generated transitions. Sound stays **SFX-only, no music** throughout. Here's the focused plan.

## Priority 1 — A 3D hero shot of Maven (biggest payoff)

Give Maven real dimensional movement — a slow cinematic push-in with a confident head-turn. We splice this into the intro or the closing CTA.

**App:** Higgsfield → *Image to Video* (choose **Veo 3.1** or **Sora 2** if you want native sound effects baked in; or **Seedance / Kling** for a silent clip and we add SFX later).

**Upload:** `maven-hero-forest.png` (Maven on the brand forest background — included).

**Prompt (paste this):**

The character does a subtle idle bob and a small confident turn of the head toward camera. Soft studio key light, premium product-render look, shallow depth of field, crisp edges. Ambient sound design only — one soft low whoosh and a subtle shimmer. Absolutely no music. Vertical 9:16, 5 seconds.

**Settings:** 9:16 · 5s · if asked for audio, choose "sound effects / ambient", not music.

**Also worth trying:** the **3D Rotation** app with `maven-mascot-transparent.png` — it orbits the character in 3D. Great for a 2-second spin accent.

## Priority 2 — One or two signature transitions

Use Higgsfield's **Transitions** app at the two biggest moments only (more than that gets noisy):
1. Logo → hook ("If you can describe it…")
2. Into the CTA ("Reserve your seat")

**App:** Higgsfield → *Transitions*. Upload two frames (end of one scene, start of the next) — it morphs between them. Max 5s each, 15s total output.

**How to get the frames:** in CapCut, scrub to the last frame of a scene and the first frame of the next, export both as images — or tell me and I'll export the exact boundary frames for you.

**Pick:** a clean morph or a soft whip — skip the flashy "glitch/raven" presets; they fight the Warm Fusion calm.

---

## Priority 3 — Sound, the halal way (no music)

Higgsfield transitions are silent ("add your own audio"). For full control and a guaranteed no-music result, add **sound effects in CapCut** (free) — its **Sound Effects** tab (not the Music tab):

- **Logo sparkle** → a soft "shimmer / sparkle" SFX on the twinkle
- **Each scene change** → a gentle "whoosh" (keep them quiet and consistent)
- **"build it."** → a single soft keyboard/UI "tick" as it lands
- **The chips (₦20k / internet / laptop)** → a light "pop" per row
- **The Reserve button** → one clean "click"
- Optional bed: a very low ambient room tone — no instruments, no beat

Keep everything subtle; SFX should punctuate, not perform.
